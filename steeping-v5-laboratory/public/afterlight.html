Five registers, scored in light and in sound. Play it quietly at a nightstand, or loud on a wall.

HBA — a drone that becomes a compass. Wide and undulating at first — pitch and pan both adrift — narrowing the longer you stay, until it settles center and in tune. An echo grows alongside it, the room listening back.
